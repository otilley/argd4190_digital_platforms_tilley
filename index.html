what are emoticons?

emoticons are a way to express emotions through text. they are made up of keyboard characters and can be used to convey a wide range of feelings, from happiness and excitement to sadness and frustration. some common examples of emoticons include :) for a smile, :( for a frown, and :D for a big grin. emoticons have been around since the early days of the internet and continue to be a popular way to communicate online.
