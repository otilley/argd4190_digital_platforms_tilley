purpose

this is a website to explore emoticons and see what they look like in real life! the point of this website is to serve as a fun space for people to explore more about emoticons and their meanings. :P

what are emoticons?

emoticons are a way to express emotions through text. they are made up of keyboard characters and can be used to convey a wide range of feelings, from happiness and excitement to sadness and frustration. emoticons have been around since the early days of the internet and continue to be a popular way to communicate online.
